Methodology & Justification

How these numbers were derived — open to see full sourcing and reasoning

▼
Executive Summary

This forecast uses a blended 7-channel model combining bottom-up Google Shopping projections, a dedicated branded search channel (volume-capped with budget rollover, leveraging TJX retail halo), Klaviyo email flow modeling (with proper add-to-cart funnel and Klaviyo flow deduplication), and subscription renewal LTV (cohort-based, 2-month interval with 75% retention). Campaign ramp (PMax learning phase) and demand seasonality are modeled independently. All assumptions are grounded in (1) BHBY's actual historical Google Ads data, (2) current Shopify store performance, (3) published industry benchmarks for supplement DTC brands, and (4) verified Shopify product pricing (API pull 2026-03-18). Projections include conservative, base, and aggressive scenarios with Monte Carlo confidence intervals from 10,000 iterations. These numbers reflect incremental DTC/Shopify revenue only — retail (TJX) channel is forecasted separately.

Why Historical Campaigns Are Not Used as Shopping Benchmarks

Old campaigns were branded search — targeting people already searching "BeHappyBeYou". Not Google Shopping.

  • Branded CTR (~5.12%) is 4–6× higher than typical Shopping CTR (0.5–1.5%) — branded searchers already intend to buy.
  • Branded CVR (~8.75%) is 3–5× higher than Shopping CVR (1.5–3.5%) for the same reason.
  • Branded CPC (~$0.56) is 2–3× lower than Shopping CPC ($0.80–$1.80) — fewer competitors bid on your brand name.
  • Average spend was $373/mo — Google Shopping at $5K–$10K/mo is a completely different scale and traffic type.
  • The store was unoptimized in 2021–2022: no subscription option, higher shipping threshold, weaker PDPs, slower mobile load.

The forecast uses industry benchmarks for supplement Google Shopping — not the historical branded metrics. The historical data informs context only.

Monte Carlo Simulation — How Confidence Intervals Are Calculated

The model runs 10,000 independent simulations. Each simulation randomly samples all key variables from triangular probability distributions:

  • Triangular distribution: has a minimum (conservative), most likely value (base), and maximum (aggressive). More realistic than uniform random — values cluster around the most likely case.
  • Variables sampled: CPC, Shopping CVR, store uplift, branded CPC/CVR, branded click cap, email capture rate, add-to-cart rate, welcome CVR, cart recovery rate, win-back rate, subscription adoption.
  • Each simulation runs all 12 months independently and computes total annual revenue and units.

After 10,000 runs, results are sorted and percentiles extracted:

  • P10 (pessimistic): Only 10% of simulations performed worse than this. Your realistic downside.
  • P50 (median / most likely): Half of simulations are above, half below. The single best estimate.
  • P90 (optimistic): Only 10% of simulations performed better. Your realistic upside.
▶
Channel F: Branded Search — Separate Model for Warm Traffic

Branded search (people searching "behappybeyou" or "be happy be you gummies") is modeled as a separate channel with its own CPC, CVR, and AOV — distinct from Google Shopping:

  • CPC: $0.50–$0.90 vs Shopping $0.80–$1.80. Historical branded CPC was $0.56 in 2021-2022; adjusted for 2026 inflation.
  • CVR: 5–10% (no store uplift applied — branded buyers are already warm). Historical CVR was 8.75% on the unoptimized store.
  • AOV: $43 vs Shopping $39. Brand-familiar buyers have higher multi-pack/bundle attach rates from TJX exposure.
  • Budget allocation: $1,750/mo at $5K total, $2,500/mo at $10K. Branded search volume is finite — you can't spend more than what people search.
  • Click volume cap: 1,000–2,200 clicks/mo (historical was 667/mo). Volume grows ~2-3× from expanded retail footprint, but is still finite. Unspent branded budget auto-rolls to Shopping.

Historical performance: $6,712 total spend, 12,000 clicks, 1,050 conversions, 8.75% CVR, $0.56 CPC — on an unoptimized store. The improved store and expanded retail footprint should sustain or exceed these metrics. Volume cap ensures the model doesn't assume more brand searches than realistically occur.

▶
Channel G: Subscription Renewals — Cohort-Based LTV Model

Subscription revenue is modeled using a cohort tracking system — each month's new subscribers generate recurring revenue in future months:

  • Adoption rate: 12–25% (base 18%) of new purchasers opt for subscribe & save. Source: ReCharge/Bold Commerce — supplement brands see 15-30% with good S&S UX.
  • Renewal interval: 2 months (60-day supply per bottle). Subscriber renews automatically every 60 days.
  • Retention: 75% per cycle. Of subscribers entering cycle N, 75% renew. This gives ~3 expected renewals over lifetime (avg 8 months).
  • Renewal AOV: $38 (10% subscribe & save discount off blended initial AOV).
  • Snowball effect: Early months' subscribers keep generating renewals — by month 6-7, subscription renewals become a meaningful revenue layer.
Example: 100 subscribers acquired in Month 1 → 75 renew in Month 3 ($2,850) → 56 in Month 5 ($2,138) → 42 in Month 7 ($1,603)… Only renewals within the 12-month horizon are counted.

Subscription renewals are NOT double-counted with the initial purchase — only renewal orders (month 3+) from each cohort are included in Channel G. Units from renewals are included in inventory forecasting.

▶
What This Forecast Does NOT Include
  • Retail / TJX channel sales — forecasted separately by the retail team and stacked on top
  • Organic growth from SEO, social media, word-of-mouth, or PR
  • Returns and refunds (historically low for gummy vitamins)
  • Seasonal spikes — Black Friday, holiday gifting, New Year wellness surge
  • Price changes or new product launches
  • Meta / Facebook Ads or TikTok Ads — only Google Shopping + Branded Search + Klaviyo modeled here
  • Subscription renewals from pre-launch subscribers — only new subscribers acquired through the ad campaigns are tracked

All of the above represent additional upside not captured in this model. The forecast is intentionally conservative on what it includes.
